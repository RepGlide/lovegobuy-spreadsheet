About This Resource

Evidence patchwork and an editorial assembly mat built around a shared LoveGoBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet. It offers four evidence pieces — source, option, condition, and missing — so each clue is assembled into a re-checkable picture with every gap left visible.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official LoveGoBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by LoveGoBuy or Google. It is not a store, a product database, a QC certification, a recommendation service, an order tracker, a fee calculator, or a purchase guarantee.

The evidence patchwork method

Everything here follows four pieces. Cobalt marks the source. Apricot marks the option. Sage marks the condition. Plum marks the missing piece. Patches that look alike are not the same source or option, and a missing part stays visible — never filled with a guess.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any clue.
  4. Confirm missing pieces at their source.

Assembly example — not a product set, recommendation, verification, or saved record.
