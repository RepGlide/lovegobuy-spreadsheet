A Complete Picture Needs Four Pieces

Four Pieces, Each Kept Separate

A clue only becomes a re-checkable picture when its source, option, condition, and missing parts are each kept visible.

Cobalt

Source patch

Where the clue came from: the sheet, a category entry, or the current destination page.

Apricot

Option patch

The clearly visible color, size, version, quantity, or set, as shown right now.

Sage

Condition patch

The time and page conditions under which price, images, stock, and claims appear.

Plum

Missing patch

What is still missing, conflicting, or not derivable from the current source.

Margin caption

Patches that look alike are not evidence they came from the same source or describe the same option. A missing part stays visible — never filled with a guess.

Patch by Source, Not Appearance

Look-Alikes Are Not the Same Piece

Similar shapes can hide different sources and different options.

1What appearance can tell you

  • The two patches look similar.
  • They may use similar wording or shapes.
  • A prompt to check their sources.

2What appearance cannot tell you

  • That the two came from the same source.
  • That they describe the same option.
  • Any confirmation beyond their separate records.
Option and Condition Strip

Read the Option With Its Condition

Option wording, images, and numbers only mean something inside the page conditions they appear under.

OptionThe visible color, size, version, quantity, or set — read as shown, not as recalled.
ConditionThe time and page conditions for the price, image, stock, and claims that sit next to the option.
RuleA number or image without its condition is a floating fragment — keep it attached to the page it came from.

Assembly example — not a product set, recommendation, verification, or saved record.

The Missing-Piece Frame

Keep the Cutout Empty

A missing piece is a frame with an empty cutout — not a space to fill.

Leave the gap visible

When a piece is missing, conflicting, changed on the page, or unclear at its source, leave the cutout empty and mark it as missing.

Do not fill the gap with a guess, do not copy a neighboring piece, and do not let a similar-looking piece stand in for the real one.

Leave the Gap Visible

Stop Before the Empty Space

A gap is a place to stop, not a place to guess.

  • 1

    Leave the gap visible. A missing piece stays marked as missing.

  • 2

    Do not fill it with a guess. A made-up piece is worse than an empty cutout.

  • 3

    Do not copy a look-alike. Similar patches are not the same piece.

  • 4

    Re-check at the source. Confirm the missing piece where the clue lives.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

A LoveGoBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links for use with agent shopping services. It is a pointer to browse and re-check, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official LoveGoBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by LoveGoBuy or Google.

What are the four evidence pieces?

Source patch records where the clue came from. Option patch records the clearly visible color, size, version, quantity, or set. Condition patch records the time and page conditions for price, images, stock, and claims. Missing patch records what is still missing, conflicting, or not derivable.

Do similar-looking patches mean the same source or option?

No. Patches that look alike are not evidence they came from the same source or describe the same option. Similarity is a reason to keep checking, not a reason to merge them.

What should I do when a piece is missing or conflicts?

Leave the gap visible and mark it as missing. Do not fill it with a guess. Stop the inference when the source is unclear or the page has changed.

How do I assemble a traceable research record?

Keep source, option, condition, and missing pieces separate and visible. A complete picture is one you can continue to re-check — not one where every gap has been quietly filled.
